About this table

An interactive periodic table built as part of an EdTech school project. Filter elements by year of discovery, density, atomic radius, crustal abundance, physical state, and nucleosynthetic origin — or click any element to see its atomic structure and full data sheet.

Atomic data follows IUPAC standards. Phases for synthetic elements past element 111 are theoretical predictions and remain debated in the literature.
